1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Information such as your username, email address, and account credentials when you create an account or sign in.
  • Profile information: Information you choose to add to your profile, such as your avatar, biography, and other profile details.
  • User-generated content: Games, images, comments, messages, and other content you create, upload, or share through supported features.
  • Communications: Information you provide when contacting our support team or communicating through Volta features.
  • Usage information: Information about how you interact with our services, including games played, features used, and technical diagnostic information, where collected.
  • Device and technical information: Information such as device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address, where necessary for security, operation, and troubleshooting.

The information actually collected depends on the features implemented in Volta and the permissions you grant.

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide game creation, publishing, and gameplay features.
  • Display profiles and content according to your settings.
  • Enable supported social and communication features.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.
  • Identify, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, and violations of our rules.
  • Respond to support requests and important service notices.
  • Improve our services and fix technical problems.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Games and Public Content

Volta may allow users to create games, customize profiles, and share content with other users.

Content you choose to publish publicly, such as games, usernames, profile pictures, and public comments, may be visible to other users. Other people may copy, save, or redistribute publicly accessible content.

Please avoid including personal information in games, usernames, profiles, or other content that you intend to make public.

If Volta provides messaging or chat features, the information you send may be processed to deliver messages, maintain the service, protect users, and investigate reported abuse.

Do not assume that messages are private or end-to-end encrypted unless the relevant feature explicitly states this. Access, storage, moderation, and retention depend on the feature and its technical implementation.

  • Safety and security: When reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, or prevent harm.
